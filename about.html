A software studio with a clear point of view

Omkar Software Studio was founded on a simple belief: good software should be understandable, dependable and genuinely useful.

Our story

How we got here

We started in Mumbai as a small group of engineers who were tired of seeing promising business ideas held back by software that did not fit. We wanted to work differently: fewer handoffs, clearer conversations and products built around real users.

That approach still guides us. Today our team supports startups, family businesses and established enterprises across a wide range of industries, from a first mobile app to complex internal systems.

We remain intentionally focused. We take on projects we can staff well and deliver with care, and we say no when a different kind of partner would serve a client better.
