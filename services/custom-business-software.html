Custom Business Software

Business software shaped around your process

Overview

Stop adapting your work to someone else's software

Off-the-shelf tools are built for an average business. Yours is not. We build systems around the exceptions, approvals and reporting your team deals with every day.

We start by documenting real workflows, then design a system that removes duplicate entry, connects departments and gives managers a trustworthy view of the business.

What's included

A clear, complete deliverable

Practical pieces that can be scoped and delivered in stages.

  • Customer relationship management systems
  • ERP and operational planning tools
  • Billing, invoicing and tax workflows
  • Inventory and warehouse tracking
  • HR, attendance and payroll support
  • Role-based permissions and audit trails
  • Dashboards, reporting and exports
  • Integration with accounting and existing tools
